How It Works

Our AI analyzes lung images using advanced deep learning algorithms

Our system uses a convolutional neural network (CNN) trained on thousands of lung CT scans and X-rays to detect patterns associated with lung cancer.

The AI model analyzes your uploaded image for common indicators of lung cancer such as nodules, masses, and other abnormalities that might be difficult to detect with the human eye.

While our system achieves high accuracy, it is designed to assist healthcare professionals rather than replace them. All results should be verified by a qualified medical practitioner.
